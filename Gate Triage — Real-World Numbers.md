
## 1. Demurrage / Free Time

### Port of Los Angeles

- Inbound foreign/offshore container free time: **4 days**
    
- 20-ft container demurrage:
    
    - Day 1–5 after free time: **$26.36/day**
        
    - Day 6–10: **$52.67/day**
        
    - Day 11+: **$105.34/day**
        
- 40-ft container demurrage:
    
    - Day 1–5: **$52.77/day**
        
    - Day 6–10: **$105.47/day**
        
    - Day 11+: **$210.93/day**
        

Sources:

- Port of LA Tariff No. 4: [https://portoflosangeles.org/business/tariff](https://portoflosangeles.org/business/tariff)
    
- 2026 Free Day Calendar: [https://kentico.portoflosangeles.org/getmedia/9a29b924-3271-4255-9382-45271b4027be/2026-pola-free-day-calendar](https://kentico.portoflosangeles.org/getmedia/9a29b924-3271-4255-9382-45271b4027be/2026-pola-free-day-calendar)
    

### Port of Long Beach

- Import free time is generally around **4 days**, but exact rules depend on terminal/carrier.
    
- No single universal demurrage rate; rates are usually carrier-specific.
    
- Maersk 2026 U.S. import example:
    
    - Day 1–4: **Free**
        
    - Day 5–8: **$300/day**
        
    - Day 9–13: **$345/day**
        
    - Day 14+: **$395/day**
        
- Operating reefer example:
    
    - **2 free days**
        
    - Then roughly **$490–$640/day**
        

Sources:

- TTI Demurrage: [https://www.totalterminals.com/detention-demurrage](https://www.totalterminals.com/detention-demurrage)
    
- Maersk U.S. Import Demurrage Tariff: [https://www.maersk.com/](https://www.maersk.com/)
    

> **Important note:** The **$210/day** Port of LA figure and the **$300–$600+/day** carrier figures are not contradictory. The first is a **port/wharf demurrage tariff**, while the higher figures can be **carrier-level demurrage charges**. In practice, there is no single universal demurrage rate across the entire port; actual charges depend on the port, terminal, carrier, container type, and how long the container has exceeded free time.

## 2. Cold-Chain Temperature

### Vaccines

- Many refrigerated vaccines: **2–8°C (36–46°F)**
    
- Many must **not be frozen**

## 3. CBP Holds

### Manifest Hold

- CBP places a hold when manifest/shipment information needs further review.
    
- Shipment cannot be released until CBP removes the hold.
    
- **No official fixed CBP duration**
    
- Simple correction cases are often around **1–3 business days** in industry practice.
    

### Document Review

- CBP/PGA requests supporting documents for review.
    
- Shipment cannot be released until review is completed.
    
- **No official fixed duration**
    

### Intensive Exam

- Container is physically inspected at an authorized examination facility.
    
- Shipment cannot be released until the exam is completed.
    
- Industry estimate: usually around **5–7 days**, potentially longer during congestion.
    

Sources:

- CBP ACE Cargo Release Rules: [https://www.cbp.gov/sites/default/files/2025-03/ACR%20Business%20Rules%20Document%20for%20Trade%202025_508c.pdf](https://www.cbp.gov/sites/default/files/2025-03/ACR%20Business%20Rules%20Document%20for%20Trade%202025_508c.pdf)

## 4. Real Port Disruptions

### 2024 Port of Los Angeles Power Outages

- By **August 7, 2024**, Port of LA had reported **9 power outages** that year.
    
- Effects included:
    
    - crane resets
        
    - stopped cargo flow
        
    - disrupted electric gates
        
    - recovery/recalibration time
        

Source:

- LA City Council: [https://cityclerk.lacity.org/onlinedocs/2024/24-0916_misc_8-7-24.pdf](https://cityclerk.lacity.org/onlinedocs/2024/24-0916_misc_8-7-24.pdf)
    

### July 19, 2024 — CrowdStrike Outage

- Port operations were disrupted by the global IT outage.
    
- Some operations switched to **manual processing**.
    
- Truck backlog remained after systems recovered.
    

Source:

- KNX: [https://www.audacy.com/knxnews/news/local/port-of-l-a-catching-up-after-crowdstrike-outage](https://www.audacy.com/knxnews/news/local/port-of-l-a-catching-up-after-crowdstrike-outage)
    

### January 27, 2025 — Everport Power Outage

- Power outage at Everport Terminal Services.
    
- Caused **congestion, long lines and delays**.
    

Source:

- STG Logistics: [https://www.stgusa.com/alerts/notice-power-outage-at-everport-terminal-services-in-los-angeles/](https://www.stgusa.com/alerts/notice-power-outage-at-everport-terminal-services-in-los-angeles/)
    

### July 2, 2025 — APM Terminal System Outage

- System outage at APM Terminal, Port of LA.
    
- Cameras were down.
    
- System reportedly went down after 5 a.m.
    
- Heavy congestion followed.
